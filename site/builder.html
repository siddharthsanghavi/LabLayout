Wall tool tips
  • Snaps to corners (□), wall midpoints (△), horizontal/vertical/45°, and lines up with other corners (dashed blue).
  • Type a length while drawing — 10, 10.5, 10' 6", 126" — then Enter.
  • Backspace removes the last corner. Click the first corner, double-click, right-click or press Enter to finish. Esc cancels.
  • Hold Alt to place freely.
  • Stretch a wall: drag any straight wall in or out; it snaps to the grid (Alt = free). Pick the grid size with ▦ Grid.
  • In Edit walls: drag a wall to stretch it (it clicks into line with other walls), drag a corner onto another wall to join them, drag the floor to move the whole room, drag corners, double-click a wall to add one, right-click a corner to delete, click a length to type it.
  • Fillets: click a corner (without dragging) and type a radius, or use Fillet all corners.
  • Curved walls: drag the round handle in the middle of a wall sideways, or click it and type the curve depth (0 = straight).

Mark rooms & areas

Mark offices, zones or areas inside the walls. Click one to name it and set who it belongs to.

☁ Shared inventory (optional)

Bake a Supabase connection into exported trackers so every device shares one inventory. See README for setup. The anon key ends up inside the HTML file — only share the file with people who should edit the inventory.

Library — drag onto the plan

Machines and fixtures dropped on a bench or wall group sit on it; dropped on the floor they get their own spot. On a phone, tap an item to add it to the middle of the room.
